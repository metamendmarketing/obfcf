Current Projects

See what we are working on to make Oak Bay a better place.

BC Professional Fire Fighters’ Burn Fund

The connection between firefighters and burn care runs deep. Each year, the Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation proudly supports the BC Professional Fire Fighters’ Burn Fund through contributions to both Bright Nights and the Burn Fund’s Burn Camp.

Learn More

Oak Bay High School Scoreboard

The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation was proud to support the addition of a new scoreboard at Oak Bay High School — an investment in athletics, school spirit and the experiences that bring students, families and our community together.

Learn More

CFAX Santas Anonymous

For generations, CFAX Santa’s Anonymous has helped make Christmas a little brighter for children and families throughout Greater Victoria. The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation is proud to support that work and help ensure local families experience the joy of the holiday season.

Learn More

The Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) at Victoria General Hospital

For more than a decade, firefighters across Greater Victoria have stood together in support of the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) at Victoria General Hospital, collectively pledging $500,000 to help care for vulnerable infants.

Learn More

Oak Bay High School Bursaries

The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation is proud to support local students as they take the next step in their educational journey through our annual bursary program, recognizing students who demonstrate leadership, integrity, and community service.
