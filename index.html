Dedicated to Service, Community & Compassion

The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation (OBFCF) is a registered charity operated on a 100% volunteer basis by the professional firefighters of Oak Bay, British Columbia.

Beyond emergency response, our members are dedicated to supporting programs that improve the quality of life for children, families, and seniors in our neighbourhood and throughout Greater Victoria.

Areas of Impact

How your generosity directly changes lives across our community.

🎓

Youth & Education

Providing high school bursaries, scholarships, and athletic facilities like the Oak Bay High scoreboard to help local students reach their full potential.

🏥

Healthcare & Critical Care

Pledging $500,000 to the Victoria General Hospital Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) and supporting the BC Professional Fire Fighters' Burn Fund.

🤝

Family & Community Support

Delivering holiday relief through CFAX Santas Anonymous and funding community infrastructure such as the Oak Bay Firefighters Park.

Our Annual Impact

Over $80,000 invested directly back into the community last year.
