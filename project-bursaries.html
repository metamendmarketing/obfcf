Education & Youth Leadership

Oak Bay High School Bursaries

Investing in Tomorrow's Community Leaders

The transition from secondary school to university, college, or vocational trade school represents an exciting milestone, but also carries significant financial challenges. The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation believes that cost should never prevent dedicated students from pursuing their educational dreams.

Each spring, our foundation presents annual bursaries to graduating seniors at Oak Bay High School who demonstrate exceptional dedication to community service, character, and integrity.

Bursary Philosophy & Selection Criteria

  • Community Service & Volunteerism: Recognizing students who actively give back to the Oak Bay and Greater Victoria community through volunteer work, coaching, and charity initiatives.
  • Leadership & Teamwork: Celebrating students who uplift their peers in the classroom, in athletic pursuits, and in arts or clubs.
  • Diverse Pathways: Our awards support university degrees, technical certifications, trades apprenticeships, and healthcare training programs.
  • Need-Based Consideration: Prioritizing students for whom bursary funds make an immediate, meaningful difference in accessing post-secondary studies.

How to Apply

Graduating Oak Bay High School students can apply each spring through the official Oak Bay High School Scholarship & Bursary Committee. For guidance and submission dates, students are encouraged to contact the school counselling department.
