Critical Care & Pediatric Health

Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) at Victoria General Hospital

A $500,000 Collective Pledge for Vancouver Island's Most Vulnerable Infants

For more than a decade, professional firefighters across Greater Victoria—including the Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation—have stood shoulder-to-shoulder with the Victoria Hospitals Foundation to fund critical pediatric care.

Together, local firefighter associations made a landmark collective pledge of $500,000 to the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) and Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) at Victoria General Hospital, the Island's primary referral centre for critically ill newborns and children.

Equipping Doctors & Nurses with Life-Saving Technology

  • State-of-the-Art Incubators (Giraffe Omnibeds): Providing micro-environments that control temperature, humidity, and sound, crucial for premature infants weighing as little as one pound.
  • Advanced Vital Sign Monitors: Enabling 24/7 continuous cardiac and respiratory tracking with real-time pediatric telemetry.
  • Specialized Transport Ventilators: Ensuring sick and fragile infants can be stabilized and safely transported between regional hospital centres without interrupting respiratory support.
  • Family-Centred Care Spaces: Creating quiet, private spaces where parents can bond with their babies during their hospital stay.

Local Firefighters Giving Back

As first responders, our members understand the value of every single second when saving a life. By investing in Victoria General Hospital’s NICU, we help ensure our healthcare partners have the cutting-edge tools they need to give every newborn the best possible start to life.
