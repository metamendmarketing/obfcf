Family & Community Relief

CFAX Santas Anonymous

Ensuring Every Child Experiences the Magic of the Holidays

For generations, CFAX Santa’s Anonymous has been one of Greater Victoria’s most cherished charitable traditions, ensuring that no child goes without a gift under the tree and no family goes without a festive holiday meal.

The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation is proud to stand alongside CFAX Santas Anonymous as long-time financial supporters and active, hands-on community volunteers throughout the holiday campaign.

How Oak Bay Firefighters Participate

  • Toy Drives & Financial Grants: Our members coordinate local drop-off collection points and provide major foundation grants to purchase brand-new, age-appropriate toys and warm winter essentials.
  • Food Hamper Support: Helping fund grocery gift cards and nutritious holiday hampers for families experiencing temporary financial hardship.
  • Volunteer Packing & Delivery: In the weeks leading up to Christmas, our off-duty firefighters roll up their sleeves at the Santas Anonymous depot to sort, pack, and personally deliver gifts directly to local families.

Compassion in Our Community

No family should feel forgotten during the holiday season. Through the generous support of Oak Bay residents and local businesses, we ensure that hundreds of children in our region wake up on Christmas morning with wide smiles and warm hearts.
