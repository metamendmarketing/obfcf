About Us

The Oak Bay Firefighters Charitable Foundation (OBFCF) is a registered charity operated by the dedicated professional firefighters of Oak Bay.

Established in 2012, our foundation is 100% volunteer-run. We are committed to funding vital community programs, helping local families in need, supporting youth education and athletics, and improving public spaces like Firefighters Park.

Through community donations, corporate sponsorships, and charity events, we work year-round to ensure every dollar directly benefits the health, safety, and well-being of our community.
